## 1. Description of Service

VYRO is an on-device video clipping application for Android that allows you to:

- Download YouTube videos for local processing
- Analyze video content using on-device signal processing
- Score and rank video clips automatically
- Edit and export short clips in various aspect ratios
- Monitor YouTube Live streams and capture highlight moments
- Optionally upload exported clips to your YouTube channel

All video processing occurs on your device. The App does not use cloud servers, generative AI, or remote inference.
